What changed, and why

  • Central basin. Mission Creek's seven outfalls (CSD-018…027) reported 78 discharge days over the CIWQS span — more than any other basin — yet the station that sits on them (BAY#220 Mission Creek) had no model and was folded into Southeast in the alerts app. It now has its own stage-1 model and stage-2 group; the station registry, the outfall registry, the alerts API and the signup zones all agree it drains the Central basin.
  • A discharge day is a bad day. The composition used to multiply the discharge probability by the measured same-day exceedance rate (which is well under 1 for small events). Chase's rule (2026-09-05): raw sewage entering the water is a stay-out day regardless of what the samples later showed, so x(0,·) ≡ 1; follow-up days use the measured decay. One implementation, src/models/impact.py, is shared by training and serving.
  • Regional rain. Every basin was evaluated on the two-gauge citywide mean (v3) and on its local NOAA gauge. North Shore and Central are clearly better on Downtown 047772 alone (holdout PR-AUC 0.953 vs 0.815, 0.967 vs 0.890); Westside and Southeast keep the mean. Each model records its rain source and serving feeds it the matching series: complete past days are re-based onto the NOAA gauges the models were trained on (the KSFO airport gauge was the only observed source before), today uses KSFO hourly, forecast days ECMWF.
  • The Poo Bot archive. 552 snapshots of SFPUC's own feed (Mar 2016 – Jan 2017) were ingested (data/poobot/). They pass a recall check against CIWQS Bayside events (13/15 = 87%), so the season is used for coverage and stage 2. The feed's Westside flags, however, appear one to two days after the rain (SFPUC posts the flag once operations confirm it), and the ablation shows they hurt the Westside stage-1 model — so that basin's stage 1 is fit without them. Details below.
  • Presented by zone. The forecast now leads with the four signup zones (Ocean Beach / Baker & China / North Beaches / East Beaches), each the worst of its stage-2 groups, with a basin view one click away. Zones, groups and basins all derive from shared/zones.py, src/models/groups.py and the station registry — no hand-typed lists.
  • Two new views. "What happened" shows gauge rain, every reported discharge with the beaches it posts (via the outfall registry), watcher postings and bacteria samples, per zone, for any date back to March 2016. "Model check" puts today's models' hindcast next to those actuals with hit / miss / false-alarm verdicts, the probabilities recorded at training time (holdout-fit where available), and the season scorecard.

Why Westside drops them. The archive's Westside onsets fall on 8 days (2016-10-16, 2016-11-21, 2016-11-22, 2016-12-10, 2016-12-12, 2016-12-23, 2017-01-08, 2017-01-09); several are zero-rain days one or two days after a storm (e.g. 2016-11-21 and 2016-12-12 recorded 0.00" at both gauges). The feed flags a structure when SFPUC confirms it, not when it starts — CIWQS dates events by their actual start, which is what the rain features line up with. 18 morning-snapshot onsets were re-dated to the previous rainy day; the rest could not be resolved honestly, so Westside's stage 1 uses CIWQS labels only (2018 →) and the archive still provides the season's coverage for the bay basins, the discharge context for 2016-17 samples in stage 2, and the "what happened" view.

Reading it. Discharge days are caught at 90–100% at the MODERATE line with false alarms in the 2–10% range — the rain → discharge relationship is strong and the models have it. Bacteria is a different story: elevated samples at Baker & China are mostly Lobos Creek and dry-weather runoff, not sewage, and the model does not (and should not) predict those; Ocean Beach and the bay zones catch roughly half of elevated-sample days, the rest being the same non-sewage sources. The alert system covers those via the SFPUC posting feed; the forecast is a sewage-risk forecast.

Serving changes

  • live_dashboard.py loads the Central model and volume head; composition, impact fraction and PAVA smoothing come from src/models/impact.py; basin/group/zone tables from src/models/groups.py.
  • Daily feature frames are built per rain source; complete past days are re-based onto ACIS daily totals for Downtown 047772 and Oceanside 047767 (mean for "avg" models). Each day is labelled gauges / observed (KSFO hourly, today) / mixed / forecast; the payload carries rain_by_gauge and zones.
  • New endpoints /forecast/api/actuals?date= (discharges + posted beaches, watcher postings, samples, gauge rain, per zone) and /forecast/api/scorecard?date= (training-time hindcast, labels, season scorecard). The date picker reaches back to 2016-03-19.
  • Artifacts: data/models/v4/, promoted into data/models/; scorecard.json.gz (75 KB) is read lazily by the model-check view.
