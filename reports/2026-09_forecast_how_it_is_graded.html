Caveats

  • Small numbers. 182 bad days out of sample, fourteen overflow days since training. A few storms decide a ranking; read differences of a handful of days as ties.
  • Most tail days are unsampled, so the tail is judged on the days the city happened to sample, which is mostly the East. The 2000–2020 lab export can extend the samples ruler across the whole training span; it is the next grading job.
  • One line grades the whole page while each zone's cheapest line differs. Per-zone lines are the open decision.
  • Holdout numbers flatter the weights model: its settings were chosen there. The since-training table is the fairer one.
  • Postings are precautionary. Against the signs the outfall split scores a small loss on Ocean Beach, where SFPUC posts after Sea Cliff-only overflows whose samples come back clean. The water could not settle it (one sampled case in ten years).
