Live corrections replay: synthetic feed, out of sample

The filed record standing in for the feed over every out-of-sample day: each CIWQS discharge day becomes an onset, 13% are never flagged and 60% of the rest appear a day late (the 2016-17 archive's measured imperfections), and the flag stays up on the days BeachWatch shows a CSO posting for the basin's beaches (else 2 days). Every day is recomposed with what was known by the end of the day before — the start-of-day forecast — through src/models/live_rules.py, then graded on the three rulers. Degraded variants are the mean of 5 random draws (range in grey). Cost = false alarms + 2 × misses.

This is the test the 2016-17 archive is too small for: 1144 out-of-sample days with an honest stage 1. Its price is that the feed is synthetic. Silence here means "truly no discharge" except for the dropped onsets, so the degradation is the only thing that makes the no-flag downgrade's cost visible; read live_v1_perfect_feed as an upper bound and the degraded mean as the estimate. A discharge day is never credited for its own flag (start-of-day grading), so the discharge-day ruler is fair here, but multi-day events do let a flagged first day lift the second — as a real feed would.
