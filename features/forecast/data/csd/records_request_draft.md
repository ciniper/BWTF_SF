# DRAFT — California Public Records Act requests for older discharge records (do not send without review)

Rewritten 2026-10-06. The first draft (2026-08) asked for Westside per-event data for
2013–2017 on the belief that it was never filed. It was: every Westside monthly report
from Mar 2011 carries a per-outfall, per-day table (hours + volume), now transcribed in
`pre2018/`. What CIWQS does not have:

- **Start times** of Westside discharges before Jan 2018 (the old tables are daily totals).
- **Bayside volumes** before Oct 2016 (the old tables give hours per group of outfalls).
- **Anything before Mar 2011**, when SFPUC's filings went online.
- The **annual wet-weather reports** (July–June seasons, "all the wet weather events for
  that period") that every 2011–2017 annual report says were submitted each July, and the
  **Westside Annual Status Reports** (due every Aug 30 from 2004 under the 2003 Oceanside order).

What is public elsewhere, as counts only (found 2026-10-06; good checks for anything a
request returns): per-outfall Westside counts per season 1997–98 → 2013–14 (SFPUC,
"Characterization of Westside Wet Weather Discharges and the Efficacy of CSD Controls",
2014, Table 2-1; in the EPA Environmental Appeals Board docket), per-outfall Westside
counts per season 1997–98 → 2017–18 (2019 Oceanside fact sheet, Table F-3), Bayside basin
counts per season 1998–99 → 2011–12 (2013 Bayside tentative order, Table F-4), and all
four basins per season 1998 → 2017 (SFPUC LTCP Synthesis, 2018, Table 1). EPA ECHO and
the legacy PCS data have no CSD outfalls at all.

**Where to send (verified 2026-10-06):**
- SFPUC: San Francisco's records portal, https://sanfrancisco.nextrequest.com, department
  "PUC - Public Utilities Commission". (The old address in this draft,
  sanfranciscopuc.nextrequest.com, redirects to a vendor page.)
- Regional Board: RB2-PRA-Request@Waterboards.ca.gov, (510) 622-2430, 1515 Clay St #1400,
  Oakland. They also offer file review by appointment. The 2019 fact sheet says its counts
  back to 1997–98 came from SFPUC's monthly self-monitoring reports, so paper-era reports
  may still be in the Board's files. Pre-2016 adopted orders are emailed on request.
- EPA Region 9 co-issues the Oceanside permit and may hold copies (FOIA); least certain.

## Request 1 — to SFPUC

Subject: Public Records Act request — combined sewer discharge event records and annual wet-weather reports, 1997–2017

To the Custodian of Records, San Francisco Public Utilities Commission:

Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.) and the San
Francisco Sunshine Ordinance, I request the following records:

1. Per-event combined sewer discharge (CSD) records for the Westside/Oceanside discharge
   points CSD-001 through CSD-007 (NPDES Permit No. CA0037681), from the earliest date
   available through December 31, 2017, showing for each event: date, discharge point,
   start time, duration and estimated volume. A telemetry-derived event list (for example
   from the overflow level sensors recorded in one-minute increments, as described in
   SFPUC's July 2014 report "Characterization of Westside Wet Weather Discharges and the
   Efficacy of CSD Controls") would satisfy this request.

2. Per-event discharge records for the Bayside discharge points (NPDES Permit No.
   CA0037664) from the earliest date available through September 30, 2016, with volume
   by discharge point, in the format of the revised "FINAL WW Summary" tables SFPUC filed
   in September 2023 for October 2016 – October 2017.

3. The annual wet-weather reports for each wet-weather season (July – June) from the
   earliest available through 2017–18 for both systems, and the Westside Annual Status
   Reports submitted under the 2003 Oceanside permit order (2004 onward).

Electronic copies (spreadsheet format preferred, PDF acceptable) delivered by email are
requested. If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the specific exemption
relied upon and release any reasonably segregable portions. If the records exist in
another form that would be more efficient to provide, I am glad to confer.

Thank you,
