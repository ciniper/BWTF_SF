{% call page_head("Email simulator", "How many alert emails one subscriber would have got over a past stretch: from the sender as it works today, and with a hold, a window rule and the same-day rule. Nothing here changes what is sent.") %}

Today's sender sends a subscriber one email for each minute in which any of their stations got worse: clear → posted, clear → discharge, or posted → discharge. Clears send nothing.

Hold waits that many minutes after a change and sends one email for everything that changed meanwhile. From the first change keeps the window fixed, so an alert waits at most the hold. Restart on each change sends once things have been quiet for the hold, which can wait longer.

Each email lists: today's email names only the stations that just got worse. A running list (an idea, held) adds a "Still in effect" section with the subscriber's other stations that are still posted or discharging when it goes out.

Same-day rule: a station that already had a discharge flag earlier the same Pacific day doesn't email a discharge again that day. A flag that comes back the next day emails as usual.

History is the Online Postings Timeline's: the alert log's real (not simulated) changes, with duplicate detections merged.
