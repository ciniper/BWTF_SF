Reading these numbers

"Bad" = a sample at or over the state single-sample limit (Enterococcus 104 MPN/100mL); the wet season is November–April. The dataset begins 2020-07-27 — the city publishes nothing earlier.

Weekly regime keeps only the first sample of each site-week (the city's scheduled Monday cadence) — the true week-by-week base rate, and the apples-to-apples comparison with BWTF's own weekly sampling. All samples adds the city's follow-up resamples, which cluster around contamination events and can roughly double a site's rate (Islais Creek: ~16% weekly vs ~30% all-samples over the full record) — it answers "how often did tests fail", not "how often were weeks bad". Sites marked sporadic are sampled only occasionally or reactively (often during storm events), so their rates aren't comparable to the weekly-sampled sites and they're listed separately at the bottom, unranked. Also worth knowing: the city samples no station at India Basin — the largest unsampled gap on the east shoreline, between Islais Creek and Sunnydale Cove.
Source: DataSF “Beach Water Quality” lab results (SFPUC shoreline monitoring program), fetched live.
