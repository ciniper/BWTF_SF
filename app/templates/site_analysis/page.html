Reading these numbers

"Bad" = a sample at or over the state single-sample limit (Enterococcus 104 MPN/100mL); the wet season is November–April. The city resamples after exceedances, which nudges absolute percentages up a little — it affects every site similarly, so the ranking is robust. Sites marked sporadic are sampled only occasionally or reactively (often during storm events), so their raw rates aren't comparable to the weekly-sampled sites and they're listed separately at the bottom, unranked. Also worth knowing: the city samples no station at India Basin — the largest unsampled gap on the east shoreline, between Islais Creek and Sunnydale Cove.
Source: DataSF “Beach Water Quality” lab results (SFPUC shoreline monitoring program), fetched live.
