Every combined-sewer discharge into San Francisco waters that SFPUC reported to state regulators — outfall, start, duration, and volume — extracted from monthly self-monitoring reports filed under the city's two sewer permits. This is the official record of what was actually discharged, alongside our real-time detections (SFPUC Alerts Timeline) the bacteria outcomes (Site Report Card) and the signs the public saw (Beach Postings).

The record goes back to March 2011. Until 2016–2018 the city filed daily totals instead of single events, and the Bay side reported no volumes, so older years count differently. How reporting changed, and how we check it
