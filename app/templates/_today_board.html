What the colors mean
City status

Circles and zone borders summarize the latest status available from SFPUC. Check the station’s sample date for when the water was collected.

  • No warning (green): SFPUC’s green status indicates that the last reported water quality result met California’s bacteria standards for water contact recreation. Since testing is not every day, this does not necessarily mean the water is currently clean.
  • Warning posted (orange): SFPUC can post a warning after detecting elevated bacteria within the past 24 hours or after a recent combined sewer discharge.
  • Sewage discharge (red): SFPUC flags a combined sewer discharge within the previous 24 to 72 hours.
  • No data (gray): We have no usable status for the station. SFPUC marks locations without data as not sampled or not routinely sampled.

See SFPUC’s official map and legend.

Surfrider samples

Each diamond shows the latest volunteer Enterococcus result at that site. Counts are in MPN (most probable number) per 100 mL.

  • Low bacteria (green): Below {{ ENTERO_CAUTION }}.
  • Medium bacteria (yellow): {{ ENTERO_CAUTION }} through {{ ENTERO_LIMIT }}. This is our caution range.
  • High bacteria (orange): Above {{ ENTERO_LIMIT }}, the single-sample threshold used here.
  • Over {{ STALE_AFTER_DAYS }} days old (hollow): We hide the grade color after {{ STALE_AFTER_DAYS }} days. Tap the marker for the result and date.
  • No result (solid gray): No Enterococcus reading is available for that site.

Volunteer results are supplemental. The City and Surfrider may sample different sites or dates, and official warnings come from SFPUC.
