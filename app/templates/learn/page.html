San Francisco's sewers are combined: what goes down the drain at home and the rain off the street share the same pipes. Pick how hard it is raining, or play a storm.

  1. Step1

    Homes and businesses

    Everything down the drain, and the rain off the roofs.

  2. Step2

    Storm drains

    Rain off the street.

  3. Step3

    One combined sewer

    Carries both, downhill to the shore. Over 90% of the city is served this way.

  4. Step4

    Transport/storage structures

    Ring the shoreline and hold what the plant cannot take yet.

  5. Step5

    Pumped to the plant

    From the storage's floor to a treatment plant.

  6. Step6

    Combined sewer discharge

    When storage is full, the settled water passes under a baffle that holds back floating trash, over the outlet's weir and out an outfall at the shore.

  7. Step7

    The plant's deep-water outfall

    Treated water leaves about four miles offshore for the Oceanside plant, and into the bay for the Southeast plant.
