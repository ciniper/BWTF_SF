City rows are SF Gov Open Data from {{ datasf_from }} and, before that, SFPUC's own lab export (Jan 2000 → Jul 2020). Enterococcus and E. coli were first measured in Jul 2002 at the bay stations and Oct 2003 on the ocean beaches (only total coliform before); until Aug 2020 the city ran E. coli where it now runs fecal coliform, so one of those two columns is empty depending on the year.

Each indicator is graded against its own state single-sample limit (total coliform's falls to 1,000 when fecal is over 10% of total); amber is Enterococcus in the caution tier, red is over the limit. The city's dataset carries the date but not the time, so a routine sample and a storm-day resample share a date and both are shown. Surfrider times are local. Sources: SF Gov Open Data · Surfrider BWTF (SF).
