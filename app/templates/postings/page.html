Reading these numbers

A posting is one station-advisory: a sign up at one station from the posted date through the reopened date, both inclusive, so a same-day post-and-reopen counts as one posted day. Cause is the record's own attribution: sewage discharge when a combined-sewer discharge is named as the source, rain advisory for the standing wet-weather warning, other when neither is named (mostly dry-weather bacteria). Postings are precautionary — on sampled sewage-posted days the water was over standard roughly half to four-fifths of the time — so this record grades what people were told, not what the water was; the Site Report Card grades the water.
